## Option comparison

For each option, identify the smallest viable exchange, the approval path, and how to reverse or reject it before agreement. Do not call a proposal “final” merely to create pressure.

## Role-play

1. State which facts the simulated counterparty is allowed to know.
2. Keep hidden motives uncertain; do not invent them as truth.
3. Introduce one realistic request at a time.
4. After each seller response, identify unsupported claims, authority overreach, missed questions, and unnecessary concessions.
5. End with a debrief and a draft approval request—not a simulated binding agreement.
