## Stalled deal

Distinguish a real blocker from lack of evidence:

- last buyer-confirmed objective or next step;
- last observable buyer action;
- unresolved constraint;
- changes in stakeholders, priority, or decision process;
- seller actions that may have added friction;
- one neutral validation that could close, restart, or disqualify the deal.

Do not infer interest from politeness or disinterest from silence alone.

## Bounded cohort

Define inclusion criteria before examining outcomes. Normalize time period, segment, deal type, and source completeness where possible. Report missingness and selection bias. A pattern is not a cause; require repeated evidence plus a plausible mechanism before recommending a broad process change.

Suggested comparison fields:

- outcome category;
- buyer-stated reason;
- stage and timing of decisive evidence;
- stakeholder coverage;
- validated problem and outcome;
- material product, delivery, security, legal, or commercial constraint;
- source completeness;
- alternative explanation.

## Buyer interview preparation

If a buyer interview is authorized outside this skill, prepare neutral questions that do not defend the seller or reveal a preferred answer. State the learning purpose, privacy treatment, and whether the conversation may be recorded. This skill only drafts the plan; it does not contact or schedule anyone.
