## Framework mapping

Frameworks are views of evidence, not truth generators.

- **BANT:** map budget, authority, need, and timing only when each has evidence. “No budget disclosed” is unknown, not no budget.
- **MEDDIC/MEDDPICC:** separate buyer metrics and process evidence from the rep's forecast. A named “champion” is reported until advocacy and access are observable.
- **SPICED:** retain the buyer's wording for situation, pain, impact, critical event, and decision. A seller-created deadline is not a critical event.
- **CHAMP:** do not use title or seniority as a substitute for a documented decision process.

## Status sensitivity

Before choosing a status, ask:

1. Which single unknown is most likely to change the result?
2. What evidence would reverse a disqualification or deprioritization?
3. Is the status driven by a current-stage requirement or an irrelevant future-stage field?
4. Would the same facts produce the same result for a differently named or demographically different contact?

If the last answer is uncertain, remove the proxy and reassess.
