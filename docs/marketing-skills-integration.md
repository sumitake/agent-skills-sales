# Using this pack with Marketing Skills

The best integration is additive, not a fork of overlapping top-level skills. Marketing Skills already has broad capabilities for outreach, research, collateral, operations, pricing, and offers. Duplicating those names would create ambiguous activation and split maintenance.

This pack materially improves five sales-facing seams around them.

## `prospecting` to `sales-qualification`

Marketing Skills can find and evidence candidate accounts. `sales-qualification` then evaluates a supplied named opportunity with reversible statuses, missing-data handling, disconfirming evidence, and explicit exclusion of protected traits and demographic proxies.

The improvement is not a competing prospecting engine: it prevents list-level “hot” scores or populated enrichment fields from silently becoming deal truth.

## `cold-email` to discovery or objection handling

Marketing Skills owns first-touch outbound copy. After a real buyer conversation, `sales-discovery` can identify unresolved questions and `sales-objection-handling` can establish what can be answered truthfully. Their output can become input to an email-writing skill.

The improvement is an upstream truth and authority check. Every resulting message remains a labeled draft; this pack does not send it or imply consent to contact.

## `sales-enablement` to evidence-grounded collateral

Marketing Skills owns reusable objection documents, decks, calculators, and playbooks. This pack contributes a more discriminating objection taxonomy, source/authority checks, no-fit outcomes, negotiation approval boundaries, and observed-versus-inferred buyer language.

The improvement is better raw material. The enablement skill still owns document architecture and presentation.

## `revops` to qualification and deal review

Marketing Skills owns lifecycle, scoring, routing, CRM automation, and deal-desk design. `sales-qualification` and `sales-deal-review` produce proposed statuses and process hypotheses without writing systems. They preserve missing evidence, reversibility, chronology, and alternative explanations.

The improvement is a safer analytical boundary before a human or governed RevOps workflow implements a change.

## `pricing` and `offers` to `sales-negotiation`

Marketing Skills owns portfolio-wide price, packaging, value metrics, and offer design. `sales-negotiation` applies established policy to one deal through an authority map, option comparison, and concession ledger. It never invents approval, a competing bid, or a deadline.

The improvement is deal-level authority and commitment discipline without duplicating pricing strategy.

## Why no additional copies were added

The original pack's sales-facing skills can be strengthened by draft boundaries, consent awareness, evidence labels, fairness checks, and explicit separation of system design from execution. Reissuing them under near-synonymous top-level names would make activation worse. This repository therefore supplies those improvements at the handoff points and remains independently useful for its five named jobs.
